Who knows you looked at a photo

When you open a friend's photo and keep it on screen for about a second, Nook tells the person who posted it that you have seen it. Scrolling quickly past does not count.

  • Only the person who posted sees this list. Their other friends do not, and you cannot see who has viewed other people's photos.
  • Only the first view is recorded. However many times you look again, it stays one entry; there is no view counter.
  • Nothing is recorded for your own photos.
  • The list disappears with the post when the post is deleted.

Contacts — only when you tap "Scan", and only in hashed form

Find friends from contacts is optional. When you tap Scan contacts:

  1. The app reads the phone numbers in your contacts on your device
  2. Each number is normalised and hashed with SHA-256 on the device
  3. Only the hashes are sent to the server, to be compared with the hashes of existing accounts
  4. The server returns the matching accounts and does not keep the hashes you sent

Contact names, the original phone numbers, emails, photos and everything else in your contacts never leave your device. If you do not tap Scan, the app does not read your contacts.

If you add your own number, the server keeps only its hash, never the number itself. You can remove it at any time.

An honest note on the limits of hashing: a phone number has only about 10 digits, so someone who obtained the entire table of hashes could in theory work backwards to each number. This protects against accidental leaks, not against someone holding a copy of the database. That is why no one but the app's own server can read this table.

Recovery email — your choice, and used for one thing only

A Nook account only needs a username and a password. You can add an email in Settings, and if you do, it is used for exactly one thing: sending a password reset link if you forget your password.

We do not send marketing to this email, do not share it, and your friends cannot see it. You can remove it at any time.

To be plain about the trade-off: without an email there is no way to recover your account if you forget your password — losing the password means losing the account and every photo in it. We do not keep passwords in readable form, so we cannot look it up for you either.

What we do not collect

  • Names, emails or photos from your contacts; your calendar, health or any other sensor data
  • Location coordinates. When you pick the Place or Weather chip while posting, the app asks for permission and reads your coordinates once, on the device, turns them into an area name (through Apple's or Google's map service) or a temperature (through Open-Meteo, with the coordinates rounded), and writes only that text on the photo. If you do not pick a chip, the app does not ask for your location.
  • Your browsing history or your activity in other apps
  • Payment details — every Nook Plus purchase is handled by the App Store or Google Play; we only learn "purchased" or "not purchased"
  • The numbers in What you have been doing: these are counted and kept on your device and never sent to the server

Who can see your photos

Only the people you have added as friends. This rule is enforced in the database itself, not just in the interface: every query runs as the person making it and is refused if there is no friendship.

Photos and videos have no public links. Each time you view one, the app receives a signed link that expires after one hour. Once it expires the link no longer works — even if someone copied it.

Where it is kept

Profiles, friends, messages and every data table are on Supabase infrastructure in Tokyo (ap-northeast-1). Photos, videos and voice recordings are in private storage on Cloudflare R2, in the Asia-Pacific region. If you live in Vietnam, your data is stored and processed outside Vietnam.

How long it is kept

  • Photos, videos, captions: until you delete the post or your account
  • Messages: until one of the two people deletes their account
  • Views and reactions on a post: until the post is deleted, or one of the two people deletes their account
  • Couple mode: until you turn it off or unfriend
  • Contact hashes sent when you Scan: not kept — used for a single match
  • The hash of your own number: until you remove it or delete your account
  • Spam counters: reset at the end of each time window and are not tied to your account
  • Crash reports: kept even after you delete your account, but the link to you is removed — the reports become anonymous

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account (at the bottom of the page). You must type XOA to confirm.

Deletion is permanent and immediate: your profile, every photo, video and voice recording, messages, friendships, couple mode, streaks, your Plus subscription record and device tokens. There is no backup to restore from. We keep no copy of anything apart from the anonymised crash reports described above.

If you cannot sign in to delete it yourself, write to us at the address below.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age.
